Icon Invites website + 6 invitation templates
Main site: index.html, style.css, script.js
Templates (folder "templates"): rose-palace, golden-dawn, garden-whisper, ocean-vows, midnight-noor, minimal-ivory
  shared files: template.css and template.js
1. Keep all files and the templates folder together, then open index.html.
2. In script.js and templates/template.js, change 91XXXXXXXXXX to your WhatsApp number.
3. Each template page has the couple's names, dates and venues. Edit them in that template's .html file.
4. To go online, upload everything (keep the folder structure) to Netlify, Vercel or your hosting.
